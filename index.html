Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

How long does it take to get my invite ready? +
Most invites are delivered within 24–48 hours after we receive your details and confirmation. Rush delivery (same day) is available for an extra fee.
Can I update the invite after it's published? +
Yes! You can request text changes (date, venue, names) at any time. We'll update it within a few hours. Design changes are included free within the first 7 days.
Do guests need to download an app? +
No downloads, no accounts, no hassle. Your invite is a regular website link that opens instantly on any phone or computer.
How do I share the invite with guests? +
You'll receive a short, easy-to-share link. Share it on WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, text message, or email — however you communicate with your guests.
How long does the invite stay live? +
Your invite stays live for 6 months by default, covering your event and the weeks after. Extended hosting (1 year or permanent) is available for a small fee.
What are the prices? +
Prices start at $49 for standard events and go up depending on the features you select. You'll receive a detailed quote before paying anything. No obligation to purchase.
Can the invite be in Spanish? +
Absolutely! We can design your invite fully in Spanish, fully in English, or bilingual (both). Just let us know in the form.
